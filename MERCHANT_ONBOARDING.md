# Connecting your WhatsApp — merchant guide

Takes about 2 minutes. You never copy any codes, keys, or IDs — OrderFlow
handles all of that for you behind the scenes.

## Before you start, have ready

- ✓ A WhatsApp Business number (or be ready to register one — Meta walks you through this in the same flow)
- ✓ A Facebook account you can log into
- ✓ Your phone nearby, for a one-time verification code (OTP)

## Steps

1. Open **WhatsApp** in your OrderFlow dashboard.
2. Click **Connect WhatsApp**.
3. A Facebook window opens. Log in if you're not already.
4. Choose or create your **Business Portfolio** — this is just Meta's name for your business account with them; if you've never used Meta's business tools before, you'll create a new one here.
5. Register or select the WhatsApp number you want customers to message.
6. Meta texts a verification code to that number — enter it when asked.
7. Review and accept the permissions Meta lists (these let OrderFlow send and receive messages on your behalf — nothing else).
8. The window closes on its own. Back in OrderFlow, you'll see "Connecting…" for a few seconds, then a green **WhatsApp Connected** with your number.

That's it — customers can now message that number and place orders.

## After connecting

- **Test Connection**: sends a message to your own number, so you can
  confirm everything's working without waiting for a real customer.
- **Quality Rating**: Meta's own health score for your number (Good
  standing / Needs attention / At risk). New numbers show "Not rated yet"
  until Meta has seen a few messages go through.
- **Disconnect**: stops new orders from coming in. Your past orders,
  customers, and everything else in your dashboard stay exactly as they
  are — disconnecting only affects new messages, and you can reconnect any
  time.

## If something goes wrong

- **The Facebook window closes immediately without connecting**: try again — sometimes this is just Facebook's popup being blocked by your browser. Check your browser isn't blocking pop-ups for this site.
- **"Connecting…" doesn't finish**: give it a minute; if it's still stuck, reload the WhatsApp page and try Connect WhatsApp again. If it keeps happening, contact support.
- **You already had WhatsApp connected the old way** (someone on our team set it up manually for you): nothing changes — it keeps working exactly as before, and you don't need to redo anything. See "Existing manual connections" below if you'd like to move to the self-service flow.

## Existing manual connections

If your number was connected before this self-service flow existed (someone
on the OrderFlow team set it up for you directly), it keeps working with no
action needed — it's stored the same way a self-service connection is, so
your dashboard, orders, and bot all behave identically either way. If you'd
like to switch to managing the connection yourself, disconnect from the
WhatsApp page and reconnect with **Connect WhatsApp** — everything except
the connection itself (your products, customers, and order history) stays
exactly as it was.
